# QA report — 2026-11-25

**Tested:** the `working` branch (commit `9340f3e`), production build, on a throwaway copy of the database. Real OpenAI key. WhatsApp, Telegram, Paystack and Resend were **not** live, so real message delivery and real payments were not tested.

**Verdict in one line:** the app mostly delivers its promise and looks good, but four problems must be fixed before any real customer uses it. Two of them affect money and trust.

## 1. Does it deliver the promise?

The promise (landing page): *"Every WhatsApp enquiry becomes a tracked order, with a status and an owner."*

| Part of the promise | Result |
| --- | --- |
| A customer message becomes a draft order | **Works.** "4 red ankara two-piece size L" produced a draft order linked to the chat, with the right items and total. |
| Orders have a status and an owner | **Works.** Draft → Confirmed → Paid → Fulfilled, an "Assigned to" field, and a history. |
| AI never invents things | **Mostly.** It refused a fake discount, a fake "I already paid", and a prompt-injection attack, and leaked no customer data. But it misquoted a price (see A1). |
| A person confirms | **Works.** The AI only drafts. Staff confirm. |
| Stock can't be oversold | **Works.** Two people confirming the last 3 units at the same moment: exactly one won. |
| Payments need proof | **Works** (tested earlier with signed test payments; not with real Paystack). |

## 2. Problems found, most serious first

### Must fix before real use

**A1. The AI quotes wrong prices for some products. (High, money)**
For a ₦3,500 product it said "₦35,000 each" in 5 of 8 replies, and "₦175,000" for 5 units. Prices in the ₦18,500–₦42,500 range were correct in every reply I checked.
*Cause:* the AI is given prices in kobo (350000) and is never told the unit, so it sometimes converts wrongly.
*Fix:* give the AI a ready-made text price ("₦3,500") with every product and tell it to quote only that. Add an eval case for a low-priced item.

**A2. The AI creates duplicate draft orders. (High, money)**
The customer asked for 4 items (draft ORD-1015 created), then said "yes please go ahead", and a second identical draft ORD-1016 was created. If staff confirm both, the customer is charged and stock is reserved twice.
*Fix:* before drafting, check for an open draft in the same chat with the same items, and update it instead.

**A3. The inbox does not scale. (High, performance)**
The inbox loads every conversation at once. With 3,000 conversations: 2.6 seconds on the server and a **10 MB page**, which would be unusable on a phone. Other lists (orders, customers, catalogue) are paginated and fine.
*Fix:* paginate or load the newest 50, with search.

**A4. The AI cannot answer "Where is my order?" (Medium–High, promise)**
This is the most common wholesale question. The AI replies "I can't check order status." The new customer status link now exists, so the AI could send it.
*Fix:* give the AI a tool to look up the customer's own orders (by their phone number only) and reply with status and link.

### Polish

- Dashboard alert grammar: "1 VIP wholesale accounts have mature pending balances", and jargon ("Customer Receivables").
- "Lace Evening Gown" appears twice under "Running low" with no size/colour shown, so the two rows look identical.
- Some alert text is cut off with "…" on the dashboard.
- No "skip to content" link: a keyboard user tabs through 13 menu items on every page.
- One low-contrast line of text on the dashboard; two list-markup issues on Reports and Operations (found by an automated accessibility scan).
- The AI's delivery answer begins "Yes, we can check delivery to Port Harcourt…", which sounds like a yes. No delivery policy exists for it to read.

## 3. Does it work effectively?

**What held up well**
- Sign-in: wrong password gives one generic message, so it can't be used to find out which emails exist.
- All 9 app routes redirect to login when signed out.
- Staff cannot reach Billing, Settings or Platform Admin.
- One business cannot open another's order or customer (404).
- HTML/script typed into names or search boxes is shown as text, not run.
- Bad phone numbers, negative or zero prices, decimal stock, absurd prices and 400-character names are all refused.
- Duplicate phone numbers and duplicate SKUs are refused (except case, see B8).
- Double-clicking Confirm or Mark as paid does not double-record anything.
- AI: handled pidgin ("Abeg how much be…"), haggling (held the listed price), minimum order quantity, out-of-stock, and refunds (passed to staff).
- No broken internal links except `/returns`.

**Speed** (phone, slow-4G, 4x slower CPU): every page showed content in 1.3–1.7 seconds and weighs about 210–240 KB. Server responses took 110–290 ms. This is good for a mobile-first market. It was measured with small data (see A3 for what happens with large data).

**Not tested:** real WhatsApp/Telegram delivery, a real Paystack payment, voice notes, customer photos through the AI, more than one browser tab editing the same order, and a load test with many simultaneous users.

## 4. Does it look good?

Yes, with caveats. It is clean, consistent and distinctive without looking like a template.
- **Strengths:** clear hierarchy, strong status colours, good use of space on desktop, a phone layout that is calm (bottom tab bar, card lists, scrolling filter chips), a landing page that explains the product in seconds, and the Figtree font reads cleanly. No horizontal scrolling on any of 20 pages at phone width.
- **Accessibility:** automated scan found only 2 minor issues across 13 pages (very good). Focus rings are visible on everything tested.
- **Weak spots:** the dashboard has a lot of competing blocks (cash, alert, queue, pipeline, low stock, reminders, messages) and is long on a phone; the copy has a few jargon phrases (see Polish); the catalogue shows grey placeholder icons until photos are added (expected, but it looks empty); there is no first-run guidance (B7).

## 5. Recommended order of work

1. **A1** price display fix, **A2** duplicate-draft check (both are small and protect money).
2. **A3** inbox pagination.
3. **A4** order-status tool for the AI, plus **B1** decision on WhatsApp auto-reply.
4. **B2, B3, B5, B8** (quick fixes).
5. **B6, B7** (product name and first-run guidance) before showing it to other businesses.
6. **B4** and the polish items.
7. Real WhatsApp, Telegram and Paystack tests with a pilot business.
